CLANK! CATACOMBS

About Clank! Catacombs

While we're known for our digital card and board games, Clank! A Deck Building Adventure is Dire Wolf's first physical board game! We hope you enjoy it as much as we've enjoyed making it!

Burgle your way to victory in Clank!, the deck-building adventure game. Sneak into Dragon Keep to steal precious artifacts. Delve deeper to find more valuable loot. Expand your deck, and watch your abilities grow.

One false step and —Clank! Careless noise draws the dragon's attention, and each artifact stolen increases its rage. To enjoy your plunder, you must escape the depths alive!

Get yer filthy hands off of me treasure!

Rules of Clank! Catacombs

Clank! is easy to learn, hard to master!

To familarize yourself with the rules of Clank!,
watch the video tutorial and learn from the best!

Length: 30 minutes.

Object of the game: The player who scores the most points wins and can escape the dungeon on time.

Release Date: 2022
